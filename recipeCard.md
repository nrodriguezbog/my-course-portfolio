# How to make Banana Bread

- YIELDS: 10 - 12 SERVING(S)
- PREP TIME: 10 MINS
- TOTAL TIME: 1 HR 30 MINS
- CAL/SERV: 256


## Ingredients:

- Cooking spray
- 1 1/2 cups (180 g.) all-purpose flour
- 1 tsp. baking soda
- 1/2 tsp. kosher salt
- 2 large eggs
- 3/4 cup (160 g.) packced dark brown sugar
- 6 Tbsp. unsalted butter, melted, cooled
- 2 cups (460 g.) mashed ripe bananas (from about 4 large)
- 2 tsp. pure vanilla extract

## Step By Step Instructions:

1. Get things going by preheating your oven (350°, please) and prepping your pan.
   1. Grease the inside of the pan with cooking spray.
   2. Line it with parchment paper, **leaving some overhang** on the long sides to serve as handles.
   3. Spray the parchment.
   4. Set the pan aside.
2. Now for the dry ingredients: 
   1. Flour.
   2. Baking soda.
   3. Salt.
   4. Whisk the above ingredients together in a medium bowl **until well combined.**
3. On to the wet ingredients:
   1. Mash the bananas really well with a fork in a separate medium bowl.
      > You want the consistency of the mashed bananas to be as smooth as possible so they mix evenly into the batter.
   3. Working in a large bowl, whisk the below ingredients until smooth:
      - Eggs.
      - Brown sugar.
      - Melted butter
    4. Whisk in the mashed bananas and vanilla until evenly combined.
5. Switch to a rubber spatula and gently fold the dry ingredients into the wet just until incorporated and no dry streaks remain.
   > Don’t overmix at this stage, or you’ll develop too much gluten and wind up with a dense and chewy loaf.
6. Scrape the batter into the prepared loaf pan and smooth the top with the spatula.
7. Bake the banana bread on the center oven rack until the top is golden brown.
   > With a toothpick, skewer, or cake tester inserted into the center comes out with a few moist crumbs attached, 50 to 60 minutes.
